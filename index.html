GameHub+ Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026 · App: GameHub+ (Android com.gamehub.arafa, iOS) · Developer: Ahmed Arafa (Varied Apps)

This policy explains what information GameHub+ ("the app", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have. You can play every game without creating an account.

1. Information you give us (optional account)

If you create an account to use the global leaderboard and profile, we store:

  • Email address and password (password is stored hashed by our backend provider, never readable by us)
  • Username, and optionally country, gender and a profile photo
  • Your best game results (game, level, score, time, moves) shown on the leaderboard

Your username, photo and best scores are visible to other players on the leaderboard.

2. Information stored only on your device

Settings (sound, theme), local high scores and game progress are saved on your device and are not sent to us.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID / IDFA), approximate location derived from your IP address, device information, ad interactions and diagnostic data, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first (Google consent form). You can change your choice at any time from the app menu → Privacy settings.
  • On iOS, personalised ads are only used if you allow tracking in the Apple "App Tracking Transparency" prompt.
  • Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive your payment card details.

5. Service providers

ProviderPurpose
SupabaseAccount login, profile, leaderboard and photo storage
Google AdMobAdvertising and consent management
Google Play / Apple App StoreApp distribution and in-app purchases

We do not sell your personal information. All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

6. How long we keep data

Account data is kept until you delete your account. When you delete it, your profile, photo and scores are removed immediately from our live systems.

7. Deleting your account and data

In the app: Menu → Account → Delete Account. Or follow the steps on our account deletion page.

8. Children

GameHub+ is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data. You can edit your profile in the app, delete your account at any time, or contact us.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.

11. Contact

Email: aarafa2006@gmail.com
