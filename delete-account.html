Delete your GameHub+ account

GameHub+ by Ahmed Arafa (Varied Apps)

Option 1 — In the app (instant)

  1. Open GameHub+ and sign in.
  2. Open the menu (☰) → Account.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

Option 2 — By email (if you no longer have the app)

Send an email from the address you registered with. We delete the account within 7 days and confirm by email.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your account (email and login)
  • Your profile: username, country, gender and profile photo
  • All your leaderboard scores

What is kept

Nothing is kept by us. Purchase records (e.g. "Remove Ads") are held by Google Play / Apple according to their own policies. Data saved locally on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

الطريقة 1 — من داخل التطبيق (فوري)

  1. افتح GameHub+ وسجّل الدخول.

أرسل رسالة من البريد المسجّل به وسنحذف الحساب خلال 7 أيام ونؤكد لك بالبريد.

الحساب (البريد وتسجيل الدخول)، والملف الشخصي (الاسم والدولة والجنس والصورة)، وكل نتائجك في لوحة الصدارة. لا نحتفظ بأي شيء. سجلات الشراء تحتفظ بها Google و Apple وفق سياساتهما.
